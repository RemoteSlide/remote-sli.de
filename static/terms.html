2. The service

The service connects a presentation in your browser with one or more phones or other devices, so that you can control the presentation remotely. It forwards messages between the devices in a session and does not store them. It is free of charge and requires no account.

Remote Slide is run by a single maintainer. The service is provided as it is, with no promised availability, support, or response time. Connections can drop, and the service may be interrupted, changed, or shut down at any time. Do not rely on it as the only way to control a presentation that matters.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Your session link. Anyone who has the session link or QR code can join the session, control your presentation, and see its slide previews. Share it only with people you trust, and open a new session if it may have leaked.
  • What you present. With the Chrome extension, the remotes receive screenshots of the browser tab that shows your presentation. Make sure nothing is visible there that the other participants should not see.
  • Pages you control. The bookmarklet and the extension run a script on the page you use them on. Use them only on pages and presentations you are allowed to use in this way.
  • Display names. Do not use display names that are unlawful, offensive, or that impersonate someone else.
